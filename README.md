# SAP FICO Training – 6 Week Task Portfolio

## Overview

This repository contains the work completed as part of a six-week SAP FICO learning and research program. The tasks progress from understanding SAP FICO fundamentals to configuration, real-world implementation, reporting, troubleshooting, and future trends. Each week focuses on a different aspect of SAP Financial Accounting (FI) and Controlling (CO), providing both conceptual understanding and practical business context.

---

## Week 1 – SAP FICO Fundamentals

### Objective
Understand the fundamentals of SAP FICO, including Financial Accounting (FI), Controlling (CO), major sub-modules, business processes, integration, benefits, and challenges.

### Topics Covered
- Financial Accounting (FI)
- Controlling (CO)
- General Ledger
- Accounts Payable
- Accounts Receivable
- Asset Accounting
- Cost Center Accounting
- Profit Center Accounting
- Internal Orders
- Profitability Analysis
- SAP S/4HANA Universal Journal
- Integration with MM, SD, PP, PS, and HCM

### Description
SAP FICO combines Financial Accounting and Controlling to provide an integrated financial management environment. FI handles external accounting activities such as general ledger, receivables, payables, assets, and financial reporting, while CO focuses on internal costs, profitability, budgeting, and performance analysis. The task explains how financial events are recorded in real time and how FI and CO work together to provide accurate financial and management information.

### Deliverable
A beginner-friendly overview of SAP FICO and its major components.

## Week 3 – SAP FICO Case Study

### Objective
Understand the real-world implementation of SAP FICO through a company case study.

### Company
**Coca-Cola HBC**

### Topics Covered
- Company background
- Business challenges
- Reasons for SAP implementation
- Global SAP template
- Phased implementation
- Finance and business-process integration
- Business services and process centralization
- SAP S/4HANA transformation
- Benefits
- Challenges and lessons learned

### Description
The case study examines Coca-Cola HBC's long-term SAP journey. The company adopted a global SAP template to create a common framework for accounting, planning, procurement, production, human resources, supply chain, and cost management. The implementation followed a phased approach, with the first phase covering back-office processes including finance and being deployed across 14 countries between 2003 and 2006. The case also examines the company's continued movement toward SAP S/4HANA and the importance of standardization, integration, governance, and continuous improvement.

### Deliverable
A real-world SAP FICO case study based on Coca-Cola HBC.

## Week 5 – SAP FICO Troubleshooting

### Objective
Research common SAP FICO issues and understand systematic methods for diagnosing and resolving them.

### Topics Covered
- Closed posting periods
- G/L account posting errors
- Missing cost center assignments
- Tax code errors
- Customer/vendor posting issues
- Document balancing issues
- Invalid cost centers
- Profit center errors
- Controlling area/company code issues
- Internal order settlement problems
- Plan-versus-actual discrepancies
- SAP troubleshooting transactions and tools

### Troubleshooting Method

The guide follows a structured approach:

1. Identify the exact error message.
2. Determine the affected company code, date, account, and document.
3. Check configuration and master data.
4. Identify whether the issue is related to authorization, configuration, master data, or transaction data.
5. Apply an authorized correction.
6. Retest the transaction.
7. Verify the accounting or controlling document.
8. Document the root cause and solution.

### Useful Tools and Transactions
- FB03 – Display Accounting Document
- FS00 – G/L Account
- OB52 – Posting Periods
- KS03 – Cost Center
- KO03 – Internal Order
- SU53 – Authorization Analysis
- SLG1 – Application Log
- ST22 – ABAP Dump Analysis

### Deliverable
A practical SAP FICO troubleshooting guide containing common problems, causes, diagnostic methods, solutions, and preventive measures.

- Agentic AI

### Description
The final task explores how SAP FICO is moving from traditional transaction processing toward intelligent, cloud-based, automated financial management. The future of SAP FICO is expected to place greater emphasis on real-time data, predictive insights, automation, and integrated enterprise processes.

The task also considers the changing role of SAP professionals. Rather than replacing finance professionals, automation is expected to reduce repetitive work and allow professionals to focus more on analysis, exception handling, strategic decision-making, governance, and business insights.

Future SAP professionals can benefit from developing SAP S/4HANA knowledge alongside data analytics, AI awareness, problem-solving, communication, and business-process skills.
